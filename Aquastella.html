AQUASTELLA

E profundis lux. From the depths, light.

Aquastella is the home of Aquastella Films, the In Plain Sight novels, and the Society that keeps their secrets. Our stories are restrained, intimate, and drawn from lived experience, from Hampton Roads to the shores of Guam.

The Aquastella Foundation

Giving back to the places our stories come from

The Aquastella Foundation is being formed to carry the series' themes into the real world. These are the areas it plans to support.

Sacred places

Preserving historic chapels and sites of worship before they are lost to weather and time.

Heritage of Guåhan

Supporting CHamoru culture, language and the arts on the island where Book Two is set.

Young writers

Scholarships for young storytellers in Guam and Hampton Roads, Virginia.

The Aquastella Foundation is in formation and is not yet a registered charity. It is not accepting donations at this time.
